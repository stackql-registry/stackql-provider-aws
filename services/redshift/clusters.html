clusters

Creates, updates, deletes, gets or lists a clusters resource.

Overview​

Nameclusters
TypeResource
Idaws.redshift.clusters

When you have renamed your original table, then you can pass the original name of the table as the NewTableName parameter value in the call to RestoreTableFromClusterSnapshot.
